# CL-124: Ask the owner for one approval round per batch

## Goal

The owner approves a lane's finished work in one round, and no push after that drops the approval.

## Context

The ruleset sets `dismiss_stale_reviews_on_push: true` and does not require an up-to-date branch. On 2026-10-31, lanes synced approved PRs because `main` had moved, and each push dropped Brendan's approval. The old rule kept stacks to three PRs, and asked the owner to merge the lower ones before a lane added more. That meant several approval rounds per lane.

## Steps

1. Branch `clutch/cl-124-one-approval`.
2. In `.agents/skills/deliver/references/pull-requests.md`, replace the stack-of-three rule. Before asking for approval, a lane puts its finished PRs in one `gh stack`, and `pr merge <top>` merges it in one step. After approval, push only to a PR that GitHub reports as `DIRTY`.
3. In the `run-lanes` skill, move the train check before the approval request, and drop the sync-when-`main`-moved step after it.

## Files

- `.agents/skills/deliver/references/pull-requests.md`
- `.agents/skills/run-lanes/SKILL.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- Neither page tells a lane to push to an approved `CLEAN` PR.
- Both pages say how one stack gets one approval round.
